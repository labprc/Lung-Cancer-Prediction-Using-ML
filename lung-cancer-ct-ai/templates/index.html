About the System

Why Choose LungCare AI?

Cutting-edge machine learning meets clinical imaging expertise — built for a BE Final Year Project.

EfficientNetB0 CNN

Trained on 1,226 real lung CT images using 2-phase fine-tuning with class-weight balancing to handle minority classes accurately.

Grad-CAM Explainability

See exactly which regions of your CT scan influenced the model's decision using gradient-weighted class activation mapping.

Professional PDF Reports

Download a comprehensive 1-page hospital-style report with patient details, CT images, Grad-CAM, and probability distributions.

5-Class Classification

Detects Normal, Benign, Adenocarcinoma, Large Cell, and Squamous Cell Carcinoma with confidence scoring and UNCERTAIN flag.

Classification Classes

Understanding what the CNN model detects

Normal

No cancer detected. CT scan appears within normal clinical parameters.

Non-Malignant

Benign

Abnormal tissue is present but non-cancerous in nature.

Non-Malignant

Adenocarcinoma

Most common lung cancer type. Originates in peripheral lung tissue.

Malignant

Large Cell Carcinoma

Aggressive, fast-growing tumor that can appear in any lung region.

Malignant

Squamous Cell

Arises in the flat cells lining the airways; often linked to smoking.
